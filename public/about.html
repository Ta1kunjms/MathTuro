About MathTuro LMS

MathTuro is an interactive, competency-based learning management system dedicated to elevating Grade 7 Mathematics achievement in secondary schools across Region XII and the Philippines.

🎯

Our Research Mission

Developed within the College of Education at Mindanao State University - General Santos City, MathTuro bridges modern digital pedagogy with the Department of Education's K-12 Mathematics Curriculum.

Through structured pre-test diagnostic gating, interactive video tutorials, and server-side verified assessments, the platform fosters mastery learning while preventing common math anxiety.

🏛️

Academic Partnership

Spearheaded in collaboration with LEMMA (League of Enthusiastic Math Majors and Advocates), MathTuro combines pre-service teacher innovation with verified educational research methodologies.

All instructional modules align with DepEd Order No. 8, s. 2015 standards, ensuring that classroom teachers can seamlessly track learner progress, identify learning gaps, and deliver targeted interventions.

Core Educational Architecture

📊

Diagnostic Pre-Testing

Learners complete diagnostic assessments that unlock curriculum content upon submission, establishing clear formative baseline scores without penalty.

🔒

75% Mastery Gating

Following DepEd assessment thresholds, subsequent quarter modules unlock strictly when learners achieve 75% or higher on the summative post-test (max 3 attempts).

📖

Dyslexia Accessibility

In accordance with R.A. 7277 (Magna Carta for PWDs), self-hosted OpenDyslexic typography is available with a single click across all learning interfaces.
