Privacy Policy

In compliance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (R.A. 10173), its Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR), and relevant issuances of the National Privacy Commission (NPC) of the Philippines.

Effective Date: February 2026 | Mindanao State University - General Santos City

1 Institutional Scope & Mandate

MathTuro Learning Management System is an academic research platform developed under the Mindanao State University - General Santos City (MSU-GSC) College of Education, in coordination with the League of Enthusiastic Math Majors and Advocates (LEMMA). MathTuro processes educational data exclusively for academic evaluation, student assessment, and educational research aligned with the Department of Education (DepEd) curriculum guidelines.

2 Personal Information We Collect

Pursuant to the principle of proportionality under Section 11 of R.A. 10173, MathTuro collects only necessary personal and academic data:

Student Information

  • Full Name and Email Address
  • 12-Digit Learner Reference Number (LRN)
  • Grade Level, Section, and School Year
  • Gender (for DepEd demographic tracking)
  • Quiz scores, completion timestamps, EXP

Educator & Administrator Data

  • Full Name and Institutional Email
  • Designated Role and Assigned Classes
  • Module authoring and assessment logs
  • Grading and verification audit trails

3 Legal Basis for Processing

Personal and academic information is processed under the lawful criteria set forth in Section 12 and Section 13 of R.A. 10173:

  • Consent: Given explicitly by learners, parents/legal guardians, or educators upon account registration.
  • Legitimate Academic Interest: Facilitating formative and summative mathematics assessments under DepEd Order No. 8, s. 2015.
  • Educational Research: Aggregated, anonymized learning analytics to assess pedagogy and educational software effectiveness.

4 Data Protection & Technical Safeguards

To safeguard student and educator data against accidental loss, unauthorized access, or unlawful destruction:

Row Level Security

Granular PostgreSQL policies strictly isolate student records to authorized teachers and authenticated owners.

Encrypted Transit

All transmissions are secured via TLS 1.3/HTTPS, ensuring answer keys and credentials are never exposed.

Zero Key Leakage

Assessment questions and answers are graded server-side via Security Definer RPCs; students cannot access answer keys.

5 Your Rights under R.A. 10173

Under Section 16 of the Data Privacy Act, every student and teacher enjoys the following statutory rights:

1. Right to be Informed: To understand how personal data is collected, processed, and safeguarded.

2. Right to Access: To request a copy of your personal data, quiz attempts, and learning records.

3. Right to Rectification: To dispute and request prompt correction of inaccurate or incomplete records.

4. Right to Erasure or Blocking: To request the suspension, withdrawal, or removal of your personal information from the active system.

5. Right to File a Complaint: To lodge a formal complaint with the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph) if you believe your privacy rights have been violated.
