Terms of Service

These Terms govern your access to and use of MathTuro LMS, an educational platform aligned with R.A. 10533 (Enhanced Basic Education Act of 2013), R.A. 8293 (Intellectual Property Code), and R.A. 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012).

Last Revised: February 2026 | Mindanao State University - General Santos City

1 Acceptance & Educational Purpose

By accessing MathTuro LMS, you agree to comply with these Terms of Service. MathTuro is developed as an interactive learning management system dedicated to enhancing Grade 7 Mathematics proficiency in Philippine secondary schools. Use of this platform is restricted to enrolled students, authorized educators, and academic researchers from MSU-GSC and collaborating educational institutions.

2 User Accounts & DepEd LRN Verification

Student and teacher accounts require authentic verification:

  • Student Registration: Learners must register with their genuine 12-digit Learner Reference Number (LRN) issued by the Department of Education. Student accounts remain in a pending queue until verified and approved by the institution administrator.
  • Account Security: You are strictly responsible for maintaining credential confidentiality. Sharing student or teacher accounts with unauthorized individuals is prohibited.

3 Academic Integrity & Assessment Protocol

MathTuro enforces strict progression gating under DepEd Order No. 8, s. 2015 and verified assessment rules:

Attempt Limits & Gating: Pre-tests, lesson quizzes, and post-tests enforce a strict 3-attempt maximum. Post-test module progression requires a passing threshold of $\ge 75\%$.
Anti-Cheat & Anti-Bypass: Submitting fake scores, intercepting PostgREST endpoints, running automated scripts, or attempting to extract answer keys from the server is a violation of academic integrity and will result in immediate account suspension.

Students and teachers are granted a revocable, non-exclusive license for personal educational study only. Unauthorized commercial redistribution, resale, or scraping is strictly prohibited.

5 Accessibility & Accommodations (R.A. 7277 / R.A. 9442)

Pursuant to Republic Act No. 7277, as amended by R.A. 9442 (Magna Carta for Persons with Disabilities), MathTuro LMS incorporates accessibility accommodations including the self-hosted OpenDyslexic typeface option, high-contrast visual cues, and keyboard-accessible navigation to ensure inclusive mathematics education for all learners.
